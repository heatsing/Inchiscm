# Safe Link Building Plan

Last updated: 2026-07-26

This plan defines Google-compliant authority building for inchiscm.com. It is a planning document only. Do not acquire, submit, buy, exchange, or request links during the current production-change freeze.

## Objective

Earn 5 to 10 genuinely relevant referring domains over 60 to 90 days after the site has stable useful assets. Do not use arbitrary backlink volume targets such as 30 to 80 links.

## Principles

- Earn links because a page or tool is useful.
- Keep outreach relevant to measurement, education, DIY, design, classroom, sewing, woodworking, screen sizing, or product dimensions.
- Prefer branded and natural anchors.
- Avoid manipulating anchor text.
- Keep records of outreach, response, and page relevance.
- Stop if outreach starts producing irrelevant or low-quality placements.

## Allowed strategies

| Strategy | Example targets | Best asset | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relevant measurement/calculator directories | Curated tool lists, classroom calculator resources | Converter hub, ruler tool, printable chart | Only submit to relevant, human-reviewed directories |
| Educational resource outreach | Teachers, homeschool pages, math resource lists | Printable chart, classroom reference | Must offer genuinely useful materials |
| DIY, sewing, woodworking, design resources | Measurement reference pages, workshop guides | Printable chart, ruler tool | Use contextual outreach, not mass blasts |
| Product launch communities | Indie tool communities, maker communities | Calibrated ruler or distinctive widget | Only when the product is distinctive and useful |
| Asset outreach | Pages discussing rulers, measurement, screen sizes | Ruler tool, screen guide, printable chart | Outreach should explain the specific usefulness |
| Unlinked brand mentions | Mentions of Inch is CM without link | Homepage or relevant tool | Only request attribution when mention already exists |
| Editorial resource links | Existing articles needing measurement reference | Exact tool or guide | Link should help the reader |

## Disallowed strategies

Do not use:

- paid dofollow links;
- PBNs;
- automated directory blasts;
- comment spam;
- forum profile spam;
- sitewide footer exchanges;
- irrelevant guest posts;
- exact-match anchor campaigns;
- link exchanges as the primary value proposition;
- mass AI-generated outreach.

## Phased plan

### Phase 1: Preparation

Entry conditions:

- production-change freeze has ended;
- current pages are stable and pass `npm run verify`;
- at least one linkable asset is ready or planned on an existing page.

Tasks:

- choose the asset;
- define target audiences;
- prepare a short, honest pitch;
- create a spreadsheet of only highly relevant prospects.

Success metric:

- 20 to 40 relevant prospects identified.

Stop condition:

- prospects are mostly generic directories or irrelevant sites.

### Phase 2: Soft launch outreach

Entry conditions:

- asset is live and useful;
- no AdSense distraction around the asset.

Tasks:

- contact 5 to 10 highly relevant sites first;
- ask for feedback before asking for links when appropriate;
- use branded or natural anchor suggestions only if asked.

Success metric:

- at least 1 to 3 replies or useful feedback items.

Stop condition:

- outreach receives spam complaints or low relevance responses.

### Phase 3: Resource outreach

Entry conditions:

- asset has been improved from early feedback.

Tasks:

- reach out to teachers, DIY, sewing, woodworking, and design resource pages;
- prioritize pages already linking to similar measurement resources;
- keep outreach manual and specific.

Success metric:

- 5 to 10 genuinely relevant referring domains over 60 to 90 days.

Stop condition:

- placements become irrelevant, paid, or anchor-manipulated.

### Phase 4: Maintenance

Tasks:

- monitor referring domains and link quality;
- update assets if links point to stale or weak content;
- document which outreach angles worked.

Success metric:

- links support ranking or discovery without quality risk.

## Outreach guardrails

Every outreach email should answer:

- why this recipient's audience would care;
- which page is relevant;
- what the asset does;
- why it is accurate or useful;
- that there is no payment or exchange request.

Avoid:

- "best free tool" hype;
- aggressive follow-ups;
- exact-match anchor instructions;
- sending the same pitch to unrelated sites.

## Initial prospect categories

- math teacher resource pages;
- homeschool measurement pages;
- woodworking measurement reference pages;
- sewing/craft measurement conversion pages;
- UX/design resource lists;
- screen/projector sizing resource pages;
- practical life-skills classroom pages.

The first link-building campaign should support one of the two selected assets: calibrated on-screen ruler or printable inch-to-cm chart.
