# Product and Growth Roadmap

## Phase 1: Foundation

- homepage converter
- inch and centimeter pages
- chart pages
- height converter
- screen converter
- sitemap and robots
- automated quality checks

## Phase 2: Focused length expansion

- feet to cm
- cm to feet and inches
- inches to mm
- mm to inches
- focused multi-unit length converter

Expansion remains limited to length and size conversions.

## Phase 3: Helpful guide content

- practical “how big is” pages
- screen size guides
- product dimension guides
- measuring without a ruler

Each guide must follow `CONTENT-GEO-RULES.md` and provide unique practical value.

## Phase 4: Data-driven expansion

- use GSC queries to validate demand
- improve CTR on high-impression pages
- strengthen relevant internal links
- expand only where search demand and user intent are validated

## Phase 5: Monetization

- consider AdSense only after stable organic traffic
- never place ads above the first usable converter
- avoid accidental-click layouts
- avoid ad-heavy pages and layout shift

Monetization must not weaken tool usability, content quality, or Google trust.
