# SEO Market Validation for Inch is CM

Last updated: 2026-07-26

This document validates the search market for inchiscm.com as a focused Length and Size Converter. It is a planning document only. It does not authorize new pages, metadata changes, route changes, AdSense, or link acquisition during the current production-change freeze.

## Positioning

Inch is CM should remain a trustworthy length and size conversion website, not a broad calculator portal. The approved topic set is:

- inches, centimeters, millimeters, meters, kilometers
- feet, yards, miles
- height conversion
- screen size conversion
- product dimensions and practical size references

Do not expand into weight, temperature, currency, finance, BMI, shoe size, clothing size, or unrelated calculators.

## Current GSC evidence

Observed launch evidence:

- First major 7-day Google test: 42,854 impressions, 4 clicks, average position 20.7.
- Height conversion pages produced about 28,925 impressions, roughly 66.7% of page-level impressions.
- Many height pages briefly ranked around positions 8 to 15.
- Exposure then declined sharply.
- 2026-07-22 to 2026-07-24: 73 impressions, 0 clicks, average position around 16.
- `/24-inches-in-cm` currently has the largest remaining impression share among supplied pages.

Interpretation:

- This is evidence that Google tested the site, especially the height cluster.
- The later decline is not enough evidence of a penalty because average position remained around 16.
- The safest response is observation, existing-page improvement, and data collection, not page expansion.

## SERP page-type analysis

Public SERP samples show the following winning page types:

- General inch/cm SERPs: converters, formula explanations, charts, and exact examples. Examples include RapidTables, UnitConverters, BYJU'S, Worldometer, and The Calculator Site.
- Exact value SERPs such as 24 inches to cm: exact answer pages with formula and sometimes a simple calculator. Examples include CalculateMe, CK-12, Symbolab, and single-purpose conversion pages.
- Height SERPs: dedicated feet+inches to cm converters, height tables, education/math examples, and health/record PDF charts.
- Screen SERPs: diagonal calculators that also estimate width, height, and aspect ratio. Examples include screen-size.info, OmniCalculator, projector/screen vendor tools, and aspect ratio calculators.

Implication:

- Inch is CM should compete on clarity, speed, exact answers, trustworthy methodology, and practical context.
- The site should not try to win by publishing thousands of near-duplicate number pages.
- Linkable assets will matter because many current winners have stronger domain authority, deeper link profiles, or long-lived utility pages.

## Likely monetization value

AdSense is paused until stable organic traffic exists. Future monetization value is likely best in:

- screen size and product dimension pages, where users compare devices, displays, furniture, packaging, and fit;
- height converter pages, because of proven search exposure and repeated use cases;
- core converter and chart pages, because they can receive high-volume utility traffic.

Ad placement must never appear above the first usable tool and must not create accidental-click layouts.

## Ranking difficulty factors

Likely difficulty comes from:

- established calculator domains with broad authority;
- long-lived conversion tables with backlinks;
- SERP features and instant answers that reduce clicks;
- Google testing and throttling behavior for new utility sites;
- potential template sameness if exact pages are not differentiated enough;
- limited referring domains for a new site.

Supporting SEO indicators such as keyword difficulty scores, backlink estimates, exact-match domains, and `intitle:` result counts can help prioritize research. They are not guaranteed ranking predictors. Actual query/page GSC data is the best decision input for this project.

## Major risks and assumptions

Risks:

- Publishing too many similar exact pages could look like scaled low-value content.
- Daily title/template changes during early testing could confuse evaluation.
- Weak links or spammy link building could harm trust.
- Ads introduced too early could weaken tool usability.
- Query variants could create duplicate-page temptation.

Assumptions:

- The height cluster showed real demand because it generated most early impressions.
- `/24-inches-in-cm` is currently the best exact-value signal.
- The domain needs more trust and links before competing for broad core terms.
- Existing pages should be improved before page-count expansion.

## Strategic conclusion

The market is valid, but the safe path is not mass publishing. The strongest proven opportunity is height conversion, followed by exact inch pages such as `/24-inches-in-cm`. Growth should move through stabilization, existing-page improvement, linkable assets, and then controlled expansion based on GSC data.
