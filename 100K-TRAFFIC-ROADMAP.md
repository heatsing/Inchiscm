# 100K Traffic Roadmap

Last updated: 2026-07-26

This roadmap describes how inchiscm.com could grow toward 100,000 monthly organic visits while staying compliant with Google Search policies. It is not a promise of traffic, rankings, or timing.

## Guiding rule

Traffic growth must come from usefulness, crawl safety, existing-page quality, validated expansion, and earned authority. Do not manufacture pages or links to chase traffic.

## Phase 1: Stabilization

Entry conditions:

- current production-change freeze is active;
- recent GSC data is low-volume after the first Google test;
- no confirmed technical defect is open.

Tasks:

- collect daily GSC data without overreacting;
- monitor top queries, top pages, devices, countries, indexing status;
- confirm sitemap, robots, canonicals, favicon, and internal links remain healthy;
- avoid page creation and broad template changes;
- keep AdSense paused.

Success metrics:

- sitemap remains processed;
- no growth in unintended indexed URLs;
- average position does not collapse across proven clusters;
- no crawl/indexing defect appears.

Stop conditions:

- confirmed technical SEO bug;
- broken canonical/sitemap/robots behavior;
- visible quality issue or accidental indexable duplicate.

Google-policy risks:

- daily speculative edits;
- mass indexing requests;
- changing templates before post-change data matures.

## Phase 2: Existing-page improvement

Entry conditions:

- freeze period ends;
- at least 7 days of post-change GSC top query and top page data is available;
- no technical defect is active.

Tasks:

- prioritize pages with impressions and positions 8 to 20;
- improve visible direct answers and supporting context;
- strengthen internal links around proven clusters;
- optimize `/24-inches-in-cm` only if it continues to receive impressions;
- improve height pages only from query/page evidence;
- keep page count unchanged unless a page passes creation gates.

Success metrics:

- CTR improves on pages with impressions;
- rankings stabilize or improve for existing pages;
- more queries map cleanly to current URLs;
- no cannibalization appears.

Stop conditions:

- multiple pages split the same query intent;
- exact pages start looking too repetitive;
- GSC shows indexing quality decline.

Google-policy risks:

- keyword stuffing;
- thin content expansion;
- rewriting titles too often;
- turning exact pages into doorway pages.

## Phase 3: Linkable-asset growth

Entry conditions:

- at least one useful asset brief passes human-value review;
- asset can live on an existing page or a clearly validated page;
- stable organic traffic or clear GSC demand supports the asset.

Tasks:

- build the calibrated on-screen ruler or printable inch-to-cm chart first;
- place the asset where it naturally belongs;
- add methodology and limitations;
- run safe, relevant outreach only after the asset is live;
- pursue 5 to 10 genuinely relevant referring domains over 60 to 90 days.

Success metrics:

- asset earns organic usage or mentions;
- relevant domains link naturally;
- branded searches or direct visits rise;
- linked page gains impressions without quality decline.

Stop conditions:

- outreach becomes irrelevant;
- links require payment, exchange, or exact-match anchors;
- asset creates accuracy or trust risk.

Google-policy risks:

- paid dofollow links;
- automated outreach;
- link schemes;
- assets built mainly as link bait without user value.

## Phase 4: Controlled content expansion

Entry conditions:

- existing pages have stable query/page data;
- page creation gates are passed;
- at least one cluster has unmet demand that existing pages cannot satisfy.

Tasks:

- propose at most one high-intent page at a time;
- document intent, evidence, parent hub, internal links, and unique value;
- prefer improving existing pages over adding new pages;
- update `seo-page-policy.json` only after review if programmatic scope changes;
- run full validation before deployment.

Success metrics:

- new pages earn impressions without cannibalization;
- indexed count grows slowly and intentionally;
- internal links support discovery;
- existing pages do not lose query ownership.

Stop conditions:

- page count grows faster than useful traffic;
- GSC shows discovered/crawled-not-indexed pressure;
- queries split across similar pages;
- content becomes repetitive.

Google-policy risks:

- scaled content abuse;
- doorway-style exact pages;
- copied competitor content;
- low-value AI filler.

## Path to 100,000 monthly visits

A realistic path requires:

1. recovery or stabilization of height cluster visibility;
2. improved CTR for exact-value pages with impressions;
3. stronger core tool/hub rankings;
4. one or two genuinely useful linkable assets;
5. slow expansion only into validated length and size intents;
6. sustained avoidance of spammy page or link tactics.

## What not to do

- do not promise ranking dates;
- do not use arbitrary backlink targets;
- do not add pages just because a query variant exists;
- do not add AdSense before stable organic traffic;
- do not broaden into unrelated calculator categories.

The next production action after the freeze should be selected from real 7-day GSC top queries and top pages, not from the overview chart alone.
