# Content and GEO Rules

## Writing standard

Every guide or blog page should:

1. Start with a direct, snippet-friendly answer.
2. Use clear descriptive headings.
3. Include the exact formula when a calculation is involved.
4. Include practical worked examples.
5. Use comparison tables when they materially improve understanding.
6. Include a concise FAQ when it answers genuine follow-up questions.
7. Add natural internal links to relevant converters, charts, and exact pages.
8. Avoid fluff, AI-like generic filler, unsupported claims, copied wording, and unnecessary repetition.

Do not overpublish. Only publish content that supports the length and size conversion ecosystem and answers a practical measurement question.

## Approved content directions

- how to convert inches to cm
- how big is 10 inches
- how big is 12 inches
- screen size vs width and height
- laptop screen size in cm
- TV screen size in cm
- product dimensions in cm and inches
- how to measure without a ruler
- inch vs cm
- metric vs imperial units
- feet and inches to cm for height

GEO-oriented content must remain factual, easy to quote accurately, and organized around direct answers, formulas, examples, and clearly labeled units.
